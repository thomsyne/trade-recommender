AUTHORITY BOUNDARY

Facts before interpretation

Thesis is separate

Prospective probability quality is measured from the locked recommendation and its fifth later daily session. Paper activation does not decide whether the thesis was right.

Execution is separate

The paper lifecycle uses completed bid/ask candles and observed spread. Missing coverage remains missing; it is never inferred by a model.

Corrections preserve history

A revised source result creates a new assessment linked to the old one. Identical inputs are idempotent and create nothing.

Research onlyThese deterministic records are authoritative. Any later Claude narrative may explain them, but cannot alter outcomes, execution facts, cohorts, or source lineage.
