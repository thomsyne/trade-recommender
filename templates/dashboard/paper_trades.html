COST POLICY · V1

Stress uncertainty instead of fabricating precision

Optimistic

Observed bid/ask spread only. 0% adverse annual financing and 0.0 additional commission pips.

Base stress

3% adverse annual financing plus 0.5 round-turn commission pips. This is a sensitivity assumption, not an observed broker charge.

Conservative

6% adverse annual financing plus 1.0 round-turn commission pip, using the maximum possible rollover count.

OANDA applies financing to positions open at 5 p.m. New York, usually triples Wednesday for weekend settlement, and can vary rates daily by pair, direction, holidays, and account terms. Exact historical rates are unavailable here, so every assessment locks its assumptions and bounded rollover count.

FILL POLICY

Adverse when order is unknowable

Executable sides

Buys enter on ask and exit on bid; sells enter on bid and exit on ask. Spread is therefore embedded.

Hourly ordering

If target and invalidation touch in one hourly candle, invalidation wins. An intrabar target on the entry candle is not credited unless entry existed at its open.

Gaps and expiry

Adverse stop gaps fill at the opening executable quote. Unclosed positions exit at the fifth session’s executable close.

Paper onlyCosts are pip sensitivities, not exact charges or cash returns. Position sizing, CAD conversion, leverage, taxes, and broker execution remain excluded. Thesis calibration is a separate experiment.
