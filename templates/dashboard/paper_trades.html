Executable sides

Buys enter on ask and exit on bid; sells enter on bid and exit on ask. Spread is therefore embedded.

Hourly ordering

If target and invalidation touch in one hourly candle, invalidation wins. An intrabar target on the entry candle is not credited unless entry existed at its open.

Gaps and expiry

Adverse stop gaps fill at the opening executable quote. Unclosed positions exit at the fifth session’s executable close.
